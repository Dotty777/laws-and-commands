# Scripture review of the 115 flagged Torah Law records

The review resolved **97 of 115 records** under the catalogue rubric and documented **18 open interpretive questions**. It revised form or category labels in **10 records** and updated the cited excerpt in **7 records**. All 115 entries have a written decision.

[Download the updated workbook](Saints-Bible-Torah-Law-Categories-2026-11-08.xlsx) · [Decision data and original flags](scripture-review.json) · [Independent audit](INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md)

Reviewers read the cited Saints Translation verses in context, including related passages where they clarify the provision. A comparison flag was resolved when the difference could be explained by the scope of the catalogue entry, Maimonides’ enumeration or the site’s application label. An open decision names a remaining question that could affect a form, category or ceremonial area.

Deuteronomy 19:15 sets the affirmative witness threshold and the corresponding ban on relying on a single witness. Maimonides N288 enumerates the ban separately. Nehemiah 13:1–3 applies Deuteronomy 23’s rule to membership in the community, supporting the Civil reading. For the body hanging overnight in Deuteronomy 21:22–23, the Civil burial procedure is explicit and the Ritual purity overlap remains interpretive.
