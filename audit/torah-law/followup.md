## Scripture and scope

- Exodus 12:8 identifies the Passover meal and its accompaniments. Verse 9 supplies the roasting and preparation restrictions. The supporting notes for the seven-day unleavened-bread provision distinguish the six days before a seventh-day observance in Exodus 13:6 and Deuteronomy 16:8.
- Numbers 18:26–28 specifies the tithe of the tithe. Verses 29–30 address the consecrated portion. Leviticus 2:12 permits the specified firstfruits and verse 11 gives the altar restrictions.
- Deuteronomy 13:10 names the first actor after the reporting clause. Verse 11 supplies the sentence. Exodus 22:29 gives the firstborn animal on the eighth day. Leviticus 22:27 permits sacrifice from that day onward.
- The Saints reference for the fastening described in the traditional index’s N87 is Exodus 36:28. Exodus 28:22 describes the chains, and the Saints numbering skips from verse 22 to verse 29. P220’s marriage-payment provision is in Exodus 22:15–16.
- Leviticus 22:10 and 13 address holy-food eligibility and belong with the existing law as parallel Scripture. Exodus 29:33 concerns the consecration meal. The written certificate in Deuteronomy 24:1 supplies context for the operative remarriage prohibition in verse 4.
- The catalogue retains the entries on the relationships named in Leviticus 18:7–8 pending an interpretation decision. The six working years and seventh-year land rest in Exodus 23:10–11 remain separate records. The Amalek judgment remains under historical direction in view of the stated trigger in Deuteronomy 25:19.
