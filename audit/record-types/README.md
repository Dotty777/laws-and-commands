# 70-book audit of commands, instructions, commissions, and wisdom

All 70 books were screened against the Saints Translation and the current primary and supporting Scripture references. The review used chapter and catalogue comparisons, the independently reviewed Round 2 proposals, and contextual checks of selected gaps and existing records. The [decision ledger](decisions.json) records coverage methods by book, including which sections were read in full and where a screen was followed by targeted passage checks.

Commands state a charge or prohibition. Instructions give guidance or a procedure. Commissions entrust a task or role. Wisdom offers counsel, a maxim, or a deliberately instructive example. A verse can support distinct records when the speaker, audience or act differs. Narrated outcomes and promises do not become commands merely because they appear in the same passage.

Added **222** records, revised **156**, and merged **16** overlapping earlier entries. The full catalogue now contains **6,613** active study records.
