<summary>Full research notes and original quoted wording</summary>

Original Scripture: Leviticus 14:46

Transformed: Hebrews distinguishes the first covenant’s outward washings from cleansing of conscience and access to God through Christ. This entry is classified as a ritual requirement in that covenant setting; ordinary hygiene and protection from illness remain separate practical questions.

Christ and the apostles: Hebrews 9:9–14 · Hebrews 10:19–22. Teaching about the ritual system applied to its component rules.

Source passages: Leviticus 14:1–57
