> **Hebrews 10:8** — After saying above, “Sacrifices and offerings and whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin Thou didst not desire or take pleasure in”—which are offered according to law—

> **Hebrews 10:9** — He then said, “Look, I have come to do Thy will.” He takes away the first in order to establish the second.

> **Hebrews 10:10** — By this will we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.

> **Hebrews 10:11** — And every priest stands daily, ministering and often offering the same sacrifices, which can never remove sins.

> **Hebrews 10:12** — But this One, having offered one sacrifice for sins for all time, sat down at the right hand of God,

> **Hebrews 10:13** — from then on waiting until His enemies are made a footstool for His feet.

> **Hebrews 10:14** — For by one offering He has perfected for all time those who are being sanctified.

> **Hebrews 10:15** — And the Holy Spirit also testifies to us; for after saying,

> **Hebrews 10:16** — “This is the covenant I will make with them after those days, says the Lord: putting My laws upon their hearts, I will also inscribe them upon their mind,”

> **Hebrews 10:17** — He adds, “And their sins and their lawless deeds I will certainly remember no more.”

> **Hebrews 10:18** — Now where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer an offering for sin.

Fulfilled: This requirement belongs to the sacrificial order whose repeated offerings Hebrews contrasts with Christ’s once-for-all offering. The classification concerns that order as a whole; it does not invent a separate symbolic fulfillment for each ingredient or action.

Christ and the apostles: Hebrews 10:1–18. Explicit teaching about the sacrificial system.

Source passages: Leviticus 4:1–35, Leviticus 6:1–23, Leviticus 16:1–34

**Source: [Leviticus 6:19](https://staging-8e0d-waywardnightjars.wpcomstaging.com/bible/law/leviticus/chapter-6/#v6-19)**

> **Leviticus 6:19** — The priest who offers it up shall eat it. It shall be eaten in a holy place, in the court of the Tent of Testimony.

**Source: [Leviticus 6:22](https://staging-8e0d-waywardnightjars.wpcomstaging.com/bible/law/leviticus/chapter-6/#v6-22)**

> **Leviticus 6:22** — Every male among the priests shall eat them; they are most holy unto the Lord.

**Source: [Leviticus 10:17–18](https://staging-8e0d-waywardnightjars.wpcomstaging.com/bible/law/leviticus/chapter-10/#v10-17)**

> **Leviticus 10:17** — Why did you not eat the offering for sin in a holy place? For it is most holy; He gave it unto you to eat, that you might take away the sin of the congregation and make atonement for them before the Lord.

> **Leviticus 10:18** — For none of its blood was brought into the holy place. You shall eat it in front, within, in a holy place, as the Lord directed me.

**Related teaching / comparison: [Hebrews 10:1–18](https://staging-8e0d-waywardnightjars.wpcomstaging.com/bible/discipleship/hebrews/chapter-10/#v10-1)**

> **Hebrews 10:1** — For the law, having a shadow of the good things to come, not the very image of the realities, can never perfect those who approach by the same sacrifices offered continually year by year.

> **Hebrews 10:2** — Otherwise, would they not have ceased to be offered, because the worshipers, once cleansed, would no longer have any consciousness of sins?

> **Hebrews 10:3** — But in them there is a reminder of sins year by year,

> **Hebrews 10:4** — for it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.

> **Hebrews 10:5** — Therefore, coming into the world, He says, “Sacrifice and offering Thou didst not desire, but a body Thou didst prepare for Me.

> **Hebrews 10:6** — In whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin Thou didst take no pleasure.

> **Hebrews 10:7** — Then I said, ‘Look, I have come—in the scroll of the book it is written about Me—to do Thy will, O God.’”

> **Hebrews 10:8** — After saying above, “Sacrifices and offerings and whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin Thou didst not desire or take pleasure in”—which are offered according to law—

> **Hebrews 10:9** — He then said, “Look, I have come to do Thy will.” He takes away the first in order to establish the second.

> **Hebrews 10:10** — By this will we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.

> **Hebrews 10:11** — And every priest stands daily, ministering and often offering the same sacrifices, which can never remove sins.

> **Hebrews 10:12** — But this One, having offered one sacrifice for sins for all time, sat down at the right hand of God,

> **Hebrews 10:13** — from then on waiting until His enemies are made a footstool for His feet.

> **Hebrews 10:14** — For by one offering He has perfected for all time those who are being sanctified.

> **Hebrews 10:15** — And the Holy Spirit also testifies to us; for after saying,

> **Hebrews 10:16** — “This is the covenant I will make with them after those days, says the Lord: putting My laws upon their hearts, I will also inscribe them upon their mind,”

> **Hebrews 10:17** — He adds, “And their sins and their lawless deeds I will certainly remember no more.”

> **Hebrews 10:18** — Now where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer an offering for sin.

<summary>Full research notes and original quoted wording</summary>

Original Scripture: Leviticus 6:19 · Leviticus 6:22 · Leviticus 10:17–18

Fulfilled: This requirement belongs to the sacrificial order whose repeated offerings Hebrews contrasts with Christ’s once-for-all offering. The classification concerns that order as a whole; it does not invent a separate symbolic fulfillment for each ingredient or action.

Christ and the apostles: Hebrews 10:1–18. Explicit teaching about the sacrificial system.

Source passages: Leviticus 6:1–23, Leviticus 10:1–20
