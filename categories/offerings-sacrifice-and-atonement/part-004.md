> **Hebrews 10:8** — After saying above, “Sacrifices and offerings and whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin Thou didst not desire or take pleasure in”—which are offered according to law—

> **Hebrews 10:9** — He then said, “Look, I have come to do Thy will.” He takes away the first in order to establish the second.

> **Hebrews 10:10** — By this will we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.

> **Hebrews 10:11** — And every priest stands daily, ministering and often offering the same sacrifices, which can never remove sins.

> **Hebrews 10:12** — But this One, having offered one sacrifice for sins for all time, sat down at the right hand of God,

> **Hebrews 10:13** — from then on waiting until His enemies are made a footstool for His feet.

> **Hebrews 10:14** — For by one offering He has perfected for all time those who are being sanctified.

> **Hebrews 10:15** — And the Holy Spirit also testifies to us; for after saying,

> **Hebrews 10:16** — “This is the covenant I will make with them after those days, says the Lord: putting My laws upon their hearts, I will also inscribe them upon their mind,”

> **Hebrews 10:17** — He adds, “And their sins and their lawless deeds I will certainly remember no more.”

> **Hebrews 10:18** — Now where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer an offering for sin.

Christ and the apostles: Hebrews 10:1–18. Explicit teaching about the sacrificial system.

Source passages: Leviticus 6:1–23
