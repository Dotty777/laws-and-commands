Torah: Deuteronomy 23:18 (KJV 23:17)

The New Testament independently warns against intercourse with a prostitute. The moral duty concerns sexual faithfulness and freedom from exploitation, not contempt toward vulnerable people.

Read with Christ and the apostles: 1 Corinthians 6:15–20

Connection and evidence: Explicit New Testament treatment. First Corinthians explicitly addresses intercourse with a prostitute and the believer’s bodily belonging to Christ.
