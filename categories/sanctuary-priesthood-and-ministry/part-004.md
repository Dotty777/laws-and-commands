**Source: [Exodus 28:12](https://staging-8e0d-waywardnightjars.wpcomstaging.com/bible/law/exodus/chapter-28/#v28-12)**

> **Exodus 28:12** — You shall place the two stones on the shoulders of the shoulder-piece. They are memorial stones for the sons of Israel, and Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel on his two shoulders before the Lord as a memorial concerning them.

**Related teaching / comparison: [Hebrews 7:11–28](https://staging-8e0d-waywardnightjars.wpcomstaging.com/bible/discipleship/hebrews/chapter-7/#v7-11)**

> **Hebrews 7:11** — Now if perfection came through the Levitical priesthood—for on its basis the people received the law—what further need was there for another priest to arise according to the order of Melchizedek, and not to be spoken of according to the order of Aaron?

> **Hebrews 7:12** — For when the priesthood is changed, a change of law necessarily takes place as well.

> **Hebrews 7:13** — For the One concerning whom these things are said belongs to another tribe, from which no one has served at the altar.

> **Hebrews 7:14** — For it is clear that our Lord has sprung from Judah, a tribe about which Moses said nothing concerning priests.

> **Hebrews 7:15** — And it is still more evident if another priest arises according to the likeness of Melchizedek,

> **Hebrews 7:16** — who has become a priest, not according to a law of a fleshly commandment, but according to the power of an indestructible life.

> **Hebrews 7:17** — For it is testified, “Thou art a priest for the age, according to the order of Melchizedek.”

> **Hebrews 7:18** — For there is a setting aside of the preceding commandment because of its weakness and uselessness,

> **Hebrews 7:19** — for the law perfected nothing; and there is the introduction of a better hope, through which we draw near to God.

> **Hebrews 7:20** — And insofar as this was not without an oath—for those became priests without an oath,

> **Hebrews 7:21** — but He with an oath through the One who said to Him, “The Lord swore and will not change His mind: Thou art a priest for the age”—

> **Hebrews 7:22** — to that extent Jesus has also become the guarantor of a better covenant.

> **Hebrews 7:23** — And there were many of those priests, because death prevented them from continuing,

> **Hebrews 7:24** — but He, because He remains for the age, has a permanent priesthood.

> **Hebrews 7:25** — Therefore He is also able to save completely those who approach God through Him, since He always lives to intercede on their behalf.

> **Hebrews 7:26** — For such a high priest was indeed fitting for us: holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from sinners, and having become higher than the heavens.

> **Hebrews 7:27** — He has no daily need, as the high priests do, to offer sacrifices first for His own sins and then for those of the people; for He did this once for all when He offered Himself.

> **Hebrews 7:28** — For the law appoints people subject to weakness as high priests, but the word of the oath, which came after the law, appoints a Son, perfected for the age.

**Related teaching / comparison: [Hebrews 9:1–12](https://staging-8e0d-waywardnightjars.wpcomstaging.com/bible/discipleship/hebrews/chapter-9/#v9-1)**

> **Hebrews 9:1** — Now the first covenant also had regulations for worship and an earthly sanctuary.

> **Hebrews 9:2** — For a tent was prepared, the first, in which were the lampstand, the table, and the presentation of the loaves; it is called the Holy Place.

> **Hebrews 9:3** — And behind the second curtain was a tent called the Holy of Holies,

> **Hebrews 9:4** — having a golden incense altar and the ark of the covenant, covered on every side with gold. In it were a golden jar containing the manna, Aaron’s rod that had sprouted, and the tablets of the covenant.

> **Hebrews 9:5** — Above it were cherubim of glory overshadowing the atonement cover. We cannot now speak of these things in detail.

> **Hebrews 9:6** — With these things so arranged, the priests enter the first tent continually, carrying out the acts of worship.

> **Hebrews 9:7** — But into the second only the high priest enters, once in the year, not without blood, which he offers for himself and for the people’s sins of ignorance.

> **Hebrews 9:8** — By this the Holy Spirit indicates that the way into the holy places has not yet been disclosed while the first tent still has its standing.

> **Hebrews 9:9** — This is a figure for the present time, according to which both gifts and sacrifices are offered that cannot perfect the worshiper with respect to conscience.

> **Hebrews 9:10** — They concern only foods, drinks, and various washings: regulations for the flesh, imposed until a time of setting things right.

> **Hebrews 9:11** — But Christ came as high priest of the good things that have come to be, through the greater and more perfect tent, not made by hands—that is, not of this creation—

> **Hebrews 9:12** — and entered the holy places once for all, not through the blood of goats and calves but through His own blood, having obtained eternal redemption.

**Related teaching / comparison: [Hebrews 10:19–22](https://staging-8e0d-waywardnightjars.wpcomstaging.com/bible/discipleship/hebrews/chapter-10/#v10-19)**

> **Hebrews 10:19** — Therefore, brothers, since we have confidence for entry into the holy places in the blood of Jesus,

> **Hebrews 10:20** — by the new and living way He inaugurated for us through the curtain—that is, His flesh—

> **Hebrews 10:21** — and since we have a great priest over the house of God,

> **Hebrews 10:22** — let us approach with a true heart in full assurance of faith, our hearts sprinkled clean from an evil conscience and our body washed with pure water.

<summary>Full research notes and original quoted wording</summary>

Original Scripture: Exodus 28:12

Fulfilled: This belongs to the earthly sanctuary and its appointed ministry. Hebrews reads that sanctuary in relation to Christ’s priesthood and access to God through Him. Its materials, transport rules and priestly specifications are retained as original instructions, not imposed as a Christian building code.

Christ and the apostles: Hebrews 7:11–28 · Hebrews 9:1–12 · Hebrews 10:19–22. Explicit teaching about priesthood and sanctuary; application to its component rules.

Source passages: Exodus 28:1–43

Source wording · Saints Greek-English

> You shall make an oracle-piece of judgments, embroidered work, according to the pattern of the shoulder-piece, of gold, blue, purple, spun scarlet yarn, and spun fine linen.
