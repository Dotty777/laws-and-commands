> **Hebrews 7:11** — Now if perfection came through the Levitical priesthood—for on its basis the people received the law—what further need was there for another priest to arise according to the order of Melchizedek, and not to be spoken of according to the order of Aaron?

> **Hebrews 7:12** — For when the priesthood is changed, a change of law necessarily takes place as well.

> **Hebrews 7:13** — For the One concerning whom these things are said belongs to another tribe, from which no one has served at the altar.

> **Hebrews 7:14** — For it is clear that our Lord has sprung from Judah, a tribe about which Moses said nothing concerning priests.

> **Hebrews 7:15** — And it is still more evident if another priest arises according to the likeness of Melchizedek,

> **Hebrews 7:16** — who has become a priest, not according to a law of a fleshly commandment, but according to the power of an indestructible life.

> **Hebrews 7:17** — For it is testified, “Thou art a priest for the age, according to the order of Melchizedek.”

> **Hebrews 7:18** — For there is a setting aside of the preceding commandment because of its weakness and uselessness,

> **Hebrews 7:19** — for the law perfected nothing; and there is the introduction of a better hope, through which we draw near to God.

> **Hebrews 7:20** — And insofar as this was not without an oath—for those became priests without an oath,

> **Hebrews 7:21** — but He with an oath through the One who said to Him, “The Lord swore and will not change His mind: Thou art a priest for the age”—

> **Hebrews 7:22** — to that extent Jesus has also become the guarantor of a better covenant.

> **Hebrews 7:23** — And there were many of those priests, because death prevented them from continuing,

> **Hebrews 7:24** — but He, because He remains for the age, has a permanent priesthood.

> **Hebrews 7:25** — Therefore He is also able to save completely those who approach God through Him, since He always lives to intercede on their behalf.

> **Hebrews 7:26** — For such a high priest was indeed fitting for us: holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from sinners, and having become higher than the heavens.

> **Hebrews 7:27** — He has no daily need, as the high priests do, to offer sacrifices first for His own sins and then for those of the people; for He did this once for all when He offered Himself.

> **Hebrews 7:28** — For the law appoints people subject to weakness as high priests, but the word of the oath, which came after the law, appoints a Son, perfected for the age.
