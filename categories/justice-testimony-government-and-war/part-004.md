A single witness is insufficient for conviction under these provisions; the law requires two or three witnesses. Jesus and Paul use related safeguards in community discipline. The requirement does not mean an initial allegation should be ignored or that several people repeating the same rumor become independent witnesses. Investigation and fair process remain essential.

Deuteronomy 19:1 — And when the Lord your God destroys the nations whose land your God gives you, and you inherit their possession, and dwell in their cities and in their houses,

Deuteronomy 19:2 — you shall set apart three cities for yourself in the midst of your land which the Lord your God gives you.

Deuteronomy 19:3 — Mark out the way for yourself, and divide into three parts the territory of your land which the Lord your God apportions unto you; and there shall be a refuge there for every slayer.

Deuteronomy 19:4 — And this shall be the ordinance for the slayer who shall flee thither and live: whosoever strikes his neighbour unintentionally, having borne him no hatred aforetime,

Deuteronomy 19:5 — as when a man goes with his neighbour into the forest to gather wood, and his hand is jolted with the axe as he cuts the wood, and the iron slips from the wood and strikes his neighbour, so that he dies, he shall flee unto one of these cities and live;

Deuteronomy 19:6 — lest the kinsman who avenges blood pursue the slayer while his heart is hot, and overtake him, if the way is long, and strike him mortally, so that he dies, though no sentence of death is due unto him, for he bore his neighbour no hatred aforetime.

Deuteronomy 19:7 — Therefore I command you this thing, saying: You shall set apart three cities for yourself.

Deuteronomy 19:8 — And if the Lord your God enlarges your borders, as He sware unto your fathers, and the Lord gives you all the land which He promised to give unto your fathers,

Deuteronomy 19:9 — if you hearken to do all these commandments which I command you this day, to love the Lord your God, and to walk in all His ways all your days, then you shall add three cities more unto these three,

Deuteronomy 19:10 — that innocent blood may not be shed in your land which the Lord your God gives you for a possession, and that there may be none guilty of blood among you.

Deuteronomy 19:11 — But if a man hates his neighbour, and lies in wait for him, and rises up against him, and strikes him mortally, so that he dies, and flees unto one of these cities,

Deuteronomy 19:12 — then the elders of his city shall send and take him thence, and deliver him into the hands of the kinsman who avenges blood, and he shall die.

Deuteronomy 19:13 — Your eye shall not spare him; and you shall cleanse the guilt of innocent blood from Israel, and it shall be well with you.

Deuteronomy 19:14 — You shall not remove your neighbour’s boundaries, which your fathers set in your inheritance, which was allotted unto you in the land which the Lord your God gives you for a possession.

Deuteronomy 19:15 — One witness shall not suffice to testify against a man concerning any injustice, or any trespass, or any sin which he sins. At the mouth of two witnesses, and at the mouth of three witnesses, shall every matter be established.

Deuteronomy 19:16 — And if an unrighteous witness rises up against a man, accusing him of ungodliness,

Deuteronomy 19:17 — then the two men between whom is the dispute shall stand before the Lord, and before the priests, and before the judges who shall be in those days.

Deuteronomy 19:18 — And the judges shall inquire diligently; and behold, if the unrighteous witness has testified falsely, and has stood against his brother,

Deuteronomy 19:19 — then you shall do unto him as he wickedly purposed to do unto his brother; and you shall remove the evil one from among yourselves.

Deuteronomy 19:20 — And those who remain shall hear and fear, and shall no more do such an evil thing among you.
