Requirements appearing in this passage: C0020, C0114, C0180, C0264, C0283, C0284, C0705, C0706, C0707, C0708, C0709, C0710, C0711. A mixed passage can link to more than one topic or classification.

Leviticus 24:1 — And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:

Leviticus 24:2 — Command the sons of Israel to bring Me pure beaten olive oil for light, to burn a lamp continually.

Leviticus 24:3 — Outside the veil, in the Tent of Testimony, Aaron and his sons shall burn it from evening to morning before the Lord continually. This is an everlasting ordinance throughout your generations.

Leviticus 24:4 — You shall burn the lamps upon the pure lampstand before the Lord until morning.

Leviticus 24:5 — And you shall take fine flour and make twelve loaves from it. Each loaf shall contain two tenths.

Leviticus 24:6 — And you shall place them in two arrangements, six in each arrangement, on the pure table before the Lord.

Leviticus 24:7 — And you shall put pure frankincense and salt upon the arrangement. They shall be set before the Lord as a memorial for the loaves.

Leviticus 24:8 — On the Sabbath day he shall set it before the Lord continually, before the sons of Israel as an everlasting covenant.

Leviticus 24:9 — And it shall belong unto Aaron and his sons, and they shall eat these things in a holy place, for this is most holy for him from the things sacrificed unto the Lord, an everlasting ordinance.

Leviticus 24:10 — And the son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian man, went out among the sons of Israel. The Israelite woman’s son and an Israelite man fought in the camp.

Leviticus 24:11 — And the Israelite woman’s son pronounced the Name and cursed, and they brought him unto Moses. His mother’s name was Shelomith daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

Leviticus 24:12 — And they placed him in custody, to judge him by the Lord’s command.

Leviticus 24:13 — And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:

Leviticus 24:14 — Bring the one who cursed outside the camp. All who heard him shall lay their hands on his head, and the whole congregation shall stone him.

Leviticus 24:15 — And speak unto the sons of Israel, and you shall say unto them: A man who curses God shall bear sin.

Leviticus 24:16 — Let one who names the Lord’s name surely be put to death. Let the whole congregation of Israel stone him with stones. Whether resident foreigner or native-born, when he names the Lord’s name, let him die.

Leviticus 24:17 — And if a man strikes a human being and the person dies, he shall surely be put to death.

Leviticus 24:18 — And one who strikes an animal and it dies shall repay life for life.

Leviticus 24:19 — And if anyone causes an injury unto his neighbour, as he has done unto him, so shall it be done unto him in return:

Leviticus 24:20 — fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he has caused an injury unto a man, so shall it be inflicted upon him.

Leviticus 24:21 — One who strikes a human being and the person dies shall surely be put to death.

Leviticus 24:22 — There shall be one judgment for the resident foreigner and the local inhabitant, for I am the Lord your God.

Leviticus 24:23 — And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel. They brought the one who cursed outside the camp and stoned him with stones. The sons of Israel did as the Lord commanded Moses.
