Original Scripture: Exodus 23:15 · Exodus 34:18 · Leviticus 23:6

Transformed: The New Testament relates festivals and Sabbaths to Christ and warns believers against judging one another over observance. The ancient calendar and rest instructions are preserved, with their application read through Christ and conscience before God.

Christ and the apostles: Colossians 2:16–17 · Romans 14:5–6. Explicit discussion of days and observance.

Scope: Sabbath and festival observance remain disputed among Christians. This label does not declare Scripture abolished or prescribe a particular weekday of Christian worship.
