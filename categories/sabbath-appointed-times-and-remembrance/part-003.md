**Related teaching / comparison: [Hebrews 9:6–14](https://staging-8e0d-waywardnightjars.wpcomstaging.com/bible/discipleship/hebrews/chapter-9/#v9-6)**

> **Hebrews 9:6** — With these things so arranged, the priests enter the first tent continually, carrying out the acts of worship.

> **Hebrews 9:7** — But into the second only the high priest enters, once in the year, not without blood, which he offers for himself and for the people’s sins of ignorance.

> **Hebrews 9:8** — By this the Holy Spirit indicates that the way into the holy places has not yet been disclosed while the first tent still has its standing.

> **Hebrews 9:9** — This is a figure for the present time, according to which both gifts and sacrifices are offered that cannot perfect the worshiper with respect to conscience.

> **Hebrews 9:10** — They concern only foods, drinks, and various washings: regulations for the flesh, imposed until a time of setting things right.

> **Hebrews 9:11** — But Christ came as high priest of the good things that have come to be, through the greater and more perfect tent, not made by hands—that is, not of this creation—

> **Hebrews 9:12** — and entered the holy places once for all, not through the blood of goats and calves but through His own blood, having obtained eternal redemption.

> **Hebrews 9:13** — For if the blood of goats and bulls, and the ashes of a heifer sprinkling those who have been defiled, sanctify for the cleanness of the flesh,

> **Hebrews 9:14** — how much more will the blood of Christ, who through an eternal Spirit offered Himself unblemished to God, cleanse our conscience from dead works to serve the living God!

Study 64: The Day of Atonement

Related indexed requirements: C0550, C0568, C0569, C0570, C0695, C0696. These links indicate overlapping passages; they do not assert that every requirement is identical to the study’s subject.

Transformed — approaching God through the enduring high priest

Connection to Christ: Hebrews explicitly relates the annual sanctuary rite to Christ’s decisive offering and entrance; this mark does not decide every commemorative practice.

Christian response today: Draw near through Christ with repentance and confidence, hold fast to hope, and encourage love and good works in the gathered community.

Saints Translation — Law: Leviticus 16:29–34 · Leviticus 23:26–32 Christ and the apostles: Hebrews 9:6–14 · Hebrews 10:1–14 · Hebrews 9:24–28 · Hebrews 10:19–25

Ancient covenant practice: An appointed high priest enters the sanctuary in the annual atonement rite, with sacrifices and Israel’s solemn observance.

Development in Christ and the apostles: Hebrews presents Christ’s decisive offering and heavenly entrance, then calls believers to approach God through him with faith, hope, love and mutual encouragement.

Saints Translation support: Hebrews 9:24–28 · Hebrews 10:19–25

Basis and scope: Direct New Testament account of the sanctuary rite and access through Christ.

Read carefully: Fulfilled identifies Christ’s offering; Transformed identifies the resulting access and response. This does not settle every question about commemorating the day or Eucharistic theology.

What Transformed means · Browse transformed practices

Israel is instructed to observe a solemn annual day of rest and self-affliction, with atonement performed by the priest. Hebrews interprets the sanctuary rites through Christ’s once-for-all offering. Christian traditions may commemorate the day differently, but no recurring ritual should be presented as completing an insufficiency in Christ’s sacrifice.
