Original Scripture: Leviticus 23:27 · Numbers 29:8–11

Fulfilled: This requirement belongs to the sacrificial order whose repeated offerings Hebrews contrasts with Christ’s once-for-all offering. The classification concerns that order as a whole; it does not invent a separate symbolic fulfillment for each ingredient or action.

Christ and the apostles: Hebrews 10:1–18. Explicit teaching about the sacrificial system.
