A new house must have a parapet so a person does not fall and bloodguilt come on the household. The ancient roof setting is specific, while the responsibility to prevent foreseeable harm is a reasonable moral application. Care for neighbors includes practical safeguards, not merely good intentions after an avoidable injury.
