<summary>Full research notes and original quoted wording</summary>

Feed My lambs. The charge to Peter answers Moses’ prayer that the congregation not be like sheep without a shepherd, and the long complaint of the prophets against shepherds who fed themselves. Proverbs gives the pastor’s rule in a line: know thoroughly the lives of your flock.

So when they had eaten breakfast, Jesus says to Simon Peter, “Simon, son of John, do you love Me more than these?” He says to Him, “Yes, Lord, You know that I love You.” He says to him, “Feed My lambs.” He says to him again a second time, “Simon, son of John, do you love Me?” He says to Him, “Yes, Lord, You know that I love You.” He says to him, “Shepherd My sheep.” He says to him the third time, “Simon, son of John, do you love Me?” Peter was grieved because He said to him the third time, “Do you love Me?” And he said to Him, “Lord, You know all things; You know that I love You.” Jesus says to him, “Feed My sheep.
