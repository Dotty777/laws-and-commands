The Sabbath was made for man. He answers the charge from David and the holy loaves, from the rest the Law gives even to the slave and the ox, and from Hosea: mercy and not sacrifice. The righteous, says Proverbs, has compassion even on his animals.

He said to them, “Have you never read what David did when he was in need and hungry, he and those with him: how he entered God’s house in the time of Abiathar the high priest and ate the loaves of presentation, which only priests may lawfully eat, and also gave them to those who were with him?” He said to them, “The Sabbath came into being for humankind, and not humankind for the Sabbath. So the Son of Man is Lord even of the Sabbath.”

Therefore, just as the Holy Spirit says, “Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts as in the provocation, on the day of testing in the wilderness, where your fathers tested Me with a trial and saw My works for forty years. Therefore I was indignant with this generation and said, ‘They always wander in their heart, and they did not know My ways,’ as I swore in My wrath, ‘They shall certainly not enter My rest.’” Watch out, brothers, lest there be in any one of you an evil heart of unbelief, in departing from the living God.
